JEMBER's TOURIST PLACE

Papuma Beach

Pantai Papuma is a coastal attraction in Jember, famous for its white sand beaches, clear turquoise waters, and striking off-shore rock formations. Surrounded by a protected coastal forest inhabited by wildlife such as monkeys and hornbills, the beach offers a blend of marine landscape and lush greenery. Its defining feature is Siti Hinggil, a high vantage point providing panoramic views of the Indian Ocean and dramatic breaking waves against giant coral sea stacks.

Rembangan Peak

Puncak Rembangan is a cool-climate hill resort located on the slopes of Mount Argopuro, roughly 650 meters above sea level. Known for its sweeping views of Jember City from above, it serves as a popular retreat featuring tea plantations, dragon fruit farms, and colonial-era lodging like Hotel Rembangan. The area is characterized by its crisp mountain air, making it a favored spot for enjoying local dairy products, warm beverages, and night-time city lights.

Mount Gambir

Gunung Gambir is a historic tea plantation estate established during the Dutch colonial era, situated on the slopes of Mount Argopuro. The landscape features rolling green tea hills spanning hundreds of hectares, accented by wooden skywalk bridges and viewing platforms designed for visitors. Situated at a high altitude, Wisata kebun teh gunung gambir offers a fresh atmosphere, trekking trails, and camping grounds amidst the expanse of tea bushes.

Tancak Waterfall

Air Terjun Tancak is a high waterfall in Jember, plunging approximately 82 meters down a sheer rock face shrouded in dense tropical rainforest. Located at the foot of Mount Argopuro in Panti district, reaching the waterfall requires a trek through coffee plantations and jungle terrain. The cascade creates a mist-filled basin surrounded by natural cliff walls, offering an untouched ecotourism experience for hikers and nature enthusiasts.
