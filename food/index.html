JEMBER's LOCAL FOOD

Suwar Suwir

Suwar-suwir is a traditional sweet confection originating from Jember, East Java, made primarily from fermented cassava (tape) that is cooked down with sugar and milk. The mixture is stirred continuously over heat until it reaches a dense, fudge-like consistency, after which it is cooled, sliced into small rectangular blocks, and wrapped in bright, colorful paper wrappers. It features a dense, chewy texture similar to caramel or nougat, offering a distinctive sweet-and-sour flavor profile derived from the natural fermentation of the cassava root. Today, suwar-suwir is widely produced in various added flavors like chocolate, durian, and vanilla, making it one of the most iconic edible souvenirs (oleh-oleh) from the region.

Prol Tape

Prol tape is a rich, dense cake native to Jember that prominently showcases fermented cassava (tape singkong) as its core ingredient. Unlike conventional sponge cakes, it uses a minimal amount of wheat flour, resulting in a heavy, moist, and slightly crumbly texture—a characteristic reflected in its name, which derives from the Javanese word moprol (meaning to break apart easily). The cake is prepared by mashing fermented cassava with sugar, eggs, butter, and coconut milk or evaporated milk, then baking it until the top turns golden brown. Traditionally garnished with grated cheese or toasted sliced almonds, prol tape delivers a complex interplay of sweet, savory, and subtly tangy flavors with a buttery finish.

Pecel Gudeg

Pecel gudeg is a unique fusion dish signature to Jember's culinary landscape, seamlessly combining two distinct Javanese classic meals onto a single plate. The dish features gudeg—unripe jackfruit slow-cooked in coconut milk and palm sugar until tender and sweet—served alongside pecel, which consists of blanched leafy vegetables, bean sprouts, and long beans dressed in a spicy, fragrant peanut sauce. Accompanied by steamed rice and side dishes like braised chicken, hard-boiled eggs, or crispy beef lung, the combination creates an intriguing taste dynamic where the rich sweetness of the jackfruit balances the nutty, savory, and spicy notes of the peanut sauce.

Tahu Kocek

Tahu kocek is a popular street food snack from Jember, consisting of deep-fried tofu mixed directly with a freshly mashed chili paste. Small blocks of tofu are coated in a thin tapioca batter, fried until crispy on the outside while remaining tender inside, and then crushed or mashed (dikocek) in a mortar along with bird's eye chilies, garlic, salt, and seasoning.
